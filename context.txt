# 2. Project Name

**My Bad Customer**

---

# 3. Core Project Idea

The idea is to create a platform where verified business owners can share and check information about customers who have caused business-related problems.

A "Bad Customer" in this platform does not mean someone is personally bad. It refers to a customer who has caused a verified business owner a business loss, serious inconvenience, or time waste.

Examples include:

* Fraud
* Payment fraud
* Failure to make payment
* Repeated late payments
* Fake orders
* Order cancellation after causing loss
* Providing misleading information
* Other actions that caused a business owner financial loss or significant time loss

The main purpose of the platform is to help business owners make more informed decisions before doing business with a customer.

---

# 4. Initial Target Users

The platform will initially target **Business Owners only**.

It should not be limited to a specific industry.

Examples of businesses that could potentially use the platform include:

* Retail shops
* Agricultural shops
* Medicine/pesticide/herbicide shops
* Builders
* Suppliers
* Service providers
* Traders
* Wholesalers
* Other small and medium businesses

The long-term goal is to potentially expand the platform across different business sectors.

For the first version, however, the main users are:

1. Business Owners
2. Admins

Customers themselves are not platform users in the initial version.

---

# 5. Business Owner Registration and Verification

A business owner cannot immediately get full access to the platform.

The flow should be:

Business Owner registers
↓
Provides required personal and business information
↓
Account enters verification pending status
↓
Admin reviews the business information
↓
Admin verifies or rejects the business
↓
If approved, the user becomes a Verified Business Owner
↓
Verified Business Owner receives access to the platform

The purpose of this verification process is to prevent random or fake users from submitting false reports against customers.

Only verified business owners should be able to access sensitive business features such as reporting customers.

---

# 6. Customer Identification

Customers will primarily be identified using:

* Full Name
* Mobile Number

A verified business owner should be able to search for a customer using these details.

The platform may later expand the customer identity model if necessary, but the initial system should focus on these basic identifiers.

---

# 7. Customer Search

A verified business owner should be able to search for a customer.

Example:

Business Owner enters:

* Customer Name
* Mobile Number

The system searches for relevant customer records and approved reports associated with that customer.

The business owner should be able to see relevant information that helps them understand whether previous verified businesses have reported significant issues.

Sensitive evidence should not be visible to other business owners.

---

# 8. Reporting a Customer

If a verified business owner experiences a business-related issue with a customer, they should be able to submit a report.

The reporting flow should include:

1. Select Customer
2. Select Issue Type
3. Add a short written description
4. Explain what happened
5. Upload supporting evidence
6. Submit the report

Issue types may include examples such as:

* Fraud
* Payment Issue
* Late Payment
* Fake Order
* Order Cancellation
* Financial Loss
* Time Loss
* Other

The business owner should be able to write a short explanation describing what happened.

For example:

"The customer placed an order and later refused to make payment."

The exact reporting categories and final wording can be refined later.

---

# 9. Evidence System

Evidence is an important part of the platform.

When submitting a report, the business owner should be able to upload supporting evidence.

Examples may include:

* Screenshots
* Payment-related screenshots
* Bills
* Invoices
* Images
* Other relevant documents, depending on the final supported file types

The important privacy rule is:

> Uploaded evidence should only be accessible to authorized administrators.

Other business owners should not directly see the evidence files.

The system should store and manage evidence securely.

---

# 10. Admin Report Review

When a business owner submits a customer report, the report should not automatically become visible to other business owners.

The flow should be:

Business Owner submits report
↓
Report enters Pending Review status
↓
Admin reviews report details
↓
Admin reviews uploaded evidence
↓
Admin decides whether the report is valid
↓
Admin approves, rejects, or requests more information

Possible report statuses can include:

* Pending
* Under Review
* More Information Required
* Approved
* Rejected

Only approved reports should be included in the information shown to other verified business owners.

---

# 11. Admin Responsibilities

The Admin is a very important part of the platform.

The Admin should be able to:

### Business Management

* View newly registered businesses
* Review business information
* Approve businesses
* Reject businesses
* Manage verification status

### Customer Report Management

* View submitted reports
* Review issue details
* Review descriptions
* Access evidence
* Approve reports
* Reject reports
* Request additional information

### General Management

The admin panel may also eventually include:

* Dashboard
* User/business management
* Report statistics
* Search and filters
* Activity monitoring

The exact scope can be refined during development.

---

# 12. Privacy Principle

Evidence and sensitive supporting files are private.

The intended access model is:

### Business Owner Who Submitted the Report

Can view their own submitted report and its status.

### Admin

Can view:

* Report details
* Evidence
* Business details
* Review history

### Other Verified Business Owners

Can view only the information that the system is designed to expose from approved reports.

They should not have direct access to private evidence files.

# 13. Mobile App

The primary business owner application will be a mobile application.

The application should initially target Android.

However, the architecture and technology should support future iOS development without rebuilding the entire application from scratch.

The chosen technology is:

# Flutter + Dart

Flutter will be used to build the mobile application.

The goal is to maintain a largely shared codebase for:

* Android
* Future iOS application

When iOS is added later, the main Flutter application code should largely remain the same, with potential platform-specific configuration and testing.

* Users

* Customers
* Customer reports
* Report status
* Evidence metadata
* Admin reviews
* Notifications
* Other related structured data

The exact database schema, tables, relationships, and constraints can be designed during the project planning and implementation process.

---

# 17. File and Evidence Storage

The chosen file/media storage solution is:

# Cloudinary

Cloudinary will be used for uploaded evidence and other supported media files.

The general conceptual flow should be:

Business Owner selects file
↓
Flutter application uploads through the appropriate backend/storage flow
↓
Cloudinary stores the actual file
↓
The relevant file URL/reference/metadata is stored in PostgreSQL

The database should not be used as the primary storage location for large image files.

The database should store references and metadata related to the uploaded files.

Evidence access must be designed carefully because evidence should only be accessible to authorized administrators and relevant permitted users.

---

# 18. Notifications

The chosen push notification technology is:

# Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

FCM will be used for mobile push notifications.

Examples of potential notifications include:

* Business verification approved
* Business verification rejected
* Report submitted successfully
* Report approved
* Report rejected
* More information requested
* Other important status updates

The general flow may be:

Admin performs an action
↓
NestJS backend detects the relevant event
↓
Backend triggers notification
↓
Firebase Cloud Messaging
↓
Push notification delivered to the mobile device

FCM should support the mobile application for both Android and future iOS integration.
